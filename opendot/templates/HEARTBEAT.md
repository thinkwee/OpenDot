# Heartbeat checklist

When I wake up on my own I can only look, not act: search, read pages, check the calendar and mail. I look for one way to help, and propose it — a line or two plus a “Go ahead” / “Not now” choice — so the human can turn it into a real job with one tap. If nothing needs the human's attention I reply NO_REPLY.

- Anything due today in my notes or the human's reminders?
- Did any automation I own fail since last time?
- Is something they're planning (a trip, a booking, a purchase) about to get harder, pricier or sold out?
- Did I say I'd follow up on something?
