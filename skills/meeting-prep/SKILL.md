# Meeting prep

Use when the human mentions an upcoming meeting/call and wants to walk in
prepared, e.g. "I have a call with Acme Corp tomorrow, help me prep."

## Steps

1. Note what's known already: attendees, company/person names, purpose, and
   anything from MEMORY.md about prior interactions with them.
2. `web_search` for background on the company/person if external and public
   (recent news, what they do, size/stage) — don't do this for purely
   internal/personal meetings where it wouldn't make sense.
3. Put together a compact one-pager:
   - **Who**: attendees and their apparent role, one line each
   - **Why**: the stated purpose / what's at stake
   - **Background**: 3–5 bullets of relevant context you found or already knew
   - **Suggested agenda**: 3–5 items in a sensible order
   - **Open questions**: things worth asking or clarifying
4. Deliver it in chat if it's short; `publish_page` it if there's enough
   material to want it open side-by-side during the call.
5. If this kind of meeting recurs (weekly 1:1, recurring client call), offer
   to `schedule` this prep to run automatically beforehand.

## Rules

- Don't invent attendee details or company facts you couldn't find — say
  "couldn't find much public info" rather than filling gaps with guesses.
- Keep the agenda genuinely useful and specific to this meeting, not a
  generic template.
